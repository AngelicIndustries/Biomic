Copyright (c) 2017 Jordan Peck, and remains available under the MIT License.
Its full MIT grant and warranty disclaimer are retained in
YungFastNoiseRigidMulti.java.

The passage adaptation YungPassageDensity additionally preserves the original
CaveCarver/NoiseGen cubic and simplex branch semantics from that same commit.
YungFastNoiseCubicRigidMulti retains the FastNoise MIT notice in its source.
YungOpenSimplex2S relocates the upstream long-seeded OpenSimplex2S wrapper,
retaining K.jpg's original attribution; it is not substituted by the differently
seeded Terra implementation. These sources ship under their retained notices
and the applicable Better Caves LGPL-3.0 license above.

YungWaterRegionSelector ports WaterRegionController and NoiseUtils from the same
revision. YungFastNoiseSimplex2D retains the original two-dimensional FastNoise
constants, hashing and float arithmetic, with its full Jordan Peck MIT notice.
YungCaveLiquidPlan preserves source liquid altitude and null-region semantics;
its immutable per-cell rock sealing and rigid native +64 frame are explicit
Biomic adaptations, not a claim of whole upstream cave-generation parity.
