Generated by `tools/import_tartarus_terrain.py` using the same graph importer and
interpreter as the isolated SkyLands port. Original expressions are preserved;
inheritance/meta references are resolved and shared graphs deduplicated. This
intermediate is not yet a complete Nether generator: feature decoration, biome
distribution, vanilla structures, height mapping and RU/native Nether blends
still require their own implementation and verification. Existing worlds are not
changed simply by bundling these resources.

## Biomic's changes (GPL-3.0 section 5: modified, 2026-10-22)

`palette-overrides.json` is applied by `TartarusManifest` when the manifest loads; `terrain-density.json` stays as
imported. It replaces the visible grounds with ordered ramps on smooth patch noise over one base rock
(`ANEMIC_GROUND`, `FLESH_GROUND`, `CRIMSON_GROUND`, `WARPED_GROUND`, `LIFELESS_GROUND`, `FLAMING_GROUND`,
`CRYSTALLINE_GROUND`, `BASALTIC_GROUND`, `WASTING_GROUND`), takes `TENTACLES`, `TENDONS` and the Lifeless Spires' and
Corrupted Wastes' magma clusters off their schedules, edits the huge crimson and warped fungus scripts (fewer low
branches, fuller caps), and makes `FLOATING_CRYSTALS`, the huge fungi and the Flaming Hellscapes' and Torrid Crags' lava
skewers rarer. Biomic's Nether also cools magma that has no lava nearby and adds magma at the lava sea's shores.
