## Surface rendering repair — September 7, 2026

`tools/ru_reference/import_cave_models.py` now imports 21 source-model families.
The additions are `hyacinth_bloom`, all six `tall_hyacinth_stock` shapes,
`hyacinth_flowers`, and blue/pink/white magnolia flowers. The multipart importer
combines every active attachment rather than randomly choosing a face; all 64
face masks retain the source X/Y rotations, UV locks and double-sided planes.
`cave-models.json` records hashes for 140 source files and references 57 textures
(54 copied RU textures and three Minecraft/EFR bindings). Artwork is unchanged.
The existing MIT attribution applies to these model/data/artwork imports.

`RuCaveModelData`, `BlockRuCaveModel` and `RuImportedDecorationBlocks` adapt
six-direction attachment and waterlogged state to block identity plus legacy
metadata (four attachment banks, dry/wet variants). No tile entities are needed.
The always-water-filled bloom/stalk, emitted light levels, item sprites and
stalk-shape transitions follow pinned `RUBlocks`, `TallHyacinthStockBlock` and
the source item/block models. Older metadata-zero stalks recover their visible
shape from their neighbours; this does not restore missing terrain depth.

`BlockRuAshenDirt` adapts pinned `AshenDirtBlock`'s smouldering property,
fire-triggered transition and occasional flame/smoke particles. Its exact
animated `ashen_dirt_smouldering.png` and `.mcmeta` are copied through
`import_decoration_palette.py`, with hashes recorded in `decoration-palette.json`.
No random glowing ore has been substituted for the source state.

Verification: 531 canonical states round-tripped through the real Java 8 Forge
registry with zero failures (four flower families × 64 masks × dry/wet, six
stalk shapes, bloom, two ashen dirt states and ten icicle states). Geometry tests
also check every attachment plane and the six stalk models. This is block/model
verification, not a claim of modern terrain parity or every shader's behavior.

result; it is not counted as a visually verified positive spire fixture.
